# Kaizen privacy policy

Kaizen is a personal app used only by its author. It has no servers and collects nothing.

- **All data stays on your devices**, in an encrypted database protected by your passphrase.
- **Google Drive sync** (optional): Kaizen asks for access only to its own hidden app-data folder (`drive.appdata`) and your email address (to show which account is connected). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. Everything it stores there is encrypted on your device first, with a key only your paired devices have, so neither Google nor anyone else can read it.
- **No analytics, ads, tracking or third-party sharing.**
- **Removing access**: disconnect Google Drive in Settings → Sync, or revoke Kaizen at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data from Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Kaizen → Delete hidden app data) removes everything it stored there.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MJTheGreat3/Kaizen.
